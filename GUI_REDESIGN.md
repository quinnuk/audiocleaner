# AudioCleaner GUI redesign

The GUI now uses a compact desktop-first two-column layout.

- **Left column:** folders, actions, watch mode, safety/history and audio/subtitle settings.
- **Right column:** live progress, activity/status log, summary and log access.
- The left column is intentionally constrained to roughly 455–560 px so buttons do not stretch into thin bars when the window is maximised.
- The left controls are scrollable, so the interface remains usable on shorter displays.
- The right activity area expands to consume the available desktop space.
- Initial window size is approximately 1120 × 720 rather than 760 × 900.
- Existing functionality, settings and icon are retained.
- Colours remain based on the supplied AudioCleaner icon: midnight navy, purple, electric cyan, green watch mode and warm gold warnings.
